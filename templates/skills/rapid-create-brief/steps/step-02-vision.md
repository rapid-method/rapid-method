# Step 2: Vision

**Goal**: Articulate the core problem, the cost of inaction, why current solutions fall short, the proposed solution, and what makes this approach defensible.

**Previous step**: [step-01-init.md](step-01-init.md)
**Next step**: [step-03-users.md](step-03-users.md)

---

## Approach

Conversational, one question at a time. Reflect what you hear before pushing further. Capture silently anything that belongs in later sections.

## 1. Open the Conversation

> "Let's start with the foundation: the problem. What problem are you solving, and who feels it most acutely?"

## 2. Explore the Problem Space

Cover (over multiple exchanges, not all at once):

- **Who has this problem?** Push for specificity — not "everyone", a real type of person.
- **How acute is the pain?** Frequency, cost, emotional weight.
- **How do people solve it today?** Workarounds, competitor tools, manual processes.
- **What's broken with current solutions?** Where do they frustrate, fall short, or fail?
- **What's the cost of leaving this unsolved?** Status quo cost.

If the user gives generic answers, push back: "Picture one real person doing this today. What does their workflow look like, and where exactly does it break?"

## 3. Explore the Solution Space

- **High-level approach** in 2-3 sentences. Lead with the *insight*, not the feature list.
- **Key differentiator(s)**: What makes this approach defensible? What would be hard for a competitor to copy?
- **Why now?** What changed that makes this the right time? (tech shift, market shift, regulation, behavior change, cost curve)

If the user can't name a differentiator beyond "we'll do it better", push: "What specifically would a competitor need to copy that's hard for them?"

## 4. Reflect & Confirm

Summarize back:

```
Here's what I'm hearing:

- **Problem**: {1 sentence}
- **Who feels it**: {persona hint}
- **Cost of inaction**: {1 sentence}
- **Current solutions fall short because**: {1 sentence}
- **Proposed approach**: {2-3 sentences}
- **Differentiator**: {1 sentence}
- **Why now**: {1 sentence}

Does this capture it, or am I missing something?
```

Iterate until the user confirms.

## 5. Append to Brief

Fill these sections in the brief file:

- **Executive Summary** — a 2-4 sentence stub combining problem + solution + differentiator. You'll polish in step-07.
- **Vision** section:
  - Problem
  - Problem Impact
  - Why Existing Solutions Fall Short
  - Proposed Solution
  - Key Differentiators
  - Why Now

Update frontmatter: `stepsCompleted: [1, 2]`. Save.

## 6. Transition

> "Solid foundation. Now let's talk about who exactly we're building this for."
